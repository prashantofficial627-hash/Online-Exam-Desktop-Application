Exam

Instructions

  • The exam has a countdown timer. It is submitted automatically when time ends.
  • Each question has only one correct answer.
  • You can move between questions using the Next and Previous buttons.
  • Your answers are saved as you select them.
  • Do not close the application during the exam.

Proctoring Notice

During this exam the application will:

  • Ask for permission to use your webcam.
  • Switch to full-screen mode.
  • Take a snapshot with your webcam every 30 seconds and save it on this computer.

These features help to monitor the exam but cannot detect every kind of misconduct.
